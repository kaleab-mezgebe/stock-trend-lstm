Engineered Quantitative Features (12+ Technical Indicators) Mathematical Formulations

1. Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + (EMA(Gain, 14) / EMA(Loss, 14))))
Measures velocity and magnitude of directional price movements to detect overbought/oversold momentum conditions.

2. Exponential Moving Average (EMA)

EMA_t = α * P_t + (1 - α) * EMA_(t-1), where α = 2 / (N + 1)
Weighting scheme decaying exponentially with time, accelerating convergence over simple rolling averages.

3. Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)

MACD = EMA_12(Close) - EMA_26(Close) | Signal = EMA_9(MACD)
Captures the relationship between two momentum moving averages with zero-lag difference histograms.

4. Bollinger Bands (Volatility Envelope)

Upper/Lower = SMA_20(Close) ± (2.0 * σ_20(Close))
Statistical volatility bands creating dynamic standard deviation support and resistance channels.

5. Average True Range (ATR)

TR = max(H-L, |H - C_prev|, |L - C_prev|) | ATR = EMA_14(TR)
Quantifies market volatility by decomposing true price gaps and intraday ranges.

6. MinMax Sequence Normalization

x_norm = (x - min(X_train)) / (max(X_train) - min(X_train))
Strictly scales feature matrices into [0, 1] without data leakage across the holdout test boundary.
